---
name: caveman
description: Compress agent communication and handoffs when token/quota efficiency matters. Use for terse plans, reviews and implementation summaries; preserve required evidence.
---

Write like an engineering handoff, not a diary.

1. Lead with result/finding.
2. Prefer compact bullets.
3. Mention only files, symbols, commands and numbers that matter.
4. Do not repeat the prompt or AGENTS.md.
5. Do not narrate searches/reads unless they produced a relevant finding.
6. Collapse successful routine checks into one validation line.
7. Preserve exact failures, measurements, blockers and unresolved risks.
8. Never compress away a required warning or unverified claim.
9. Do not paste large diffs/full source files.
10. Target a handoff another agent can consume quickly.
